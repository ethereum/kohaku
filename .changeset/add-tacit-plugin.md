Add `@kohaku-eth/tacit`, a plugin for the Tacit EVM shielded pool: shield, private transfer and unshield of ETH on Ethereum, Base, Robinhood Chain and MegaETH. Proofs are made on the user's device with the pool's ceremony files, spends can be submitted by a relayer or sent by the user's own account, and the identity key is derived from the keystore at `m/29793'/1'/{accountIndex}'`.
