The zkPassport method refuses a domain the zkPassport stack would rewrite and checks the proof's scope words against the config. `@zkpassport/sdk` 0.17.1 trims the domain given to its constructor, strips a leading `http://` or `https://`, the last path segment, the first port, a query and a fragment, and lower-cases it; the app proves for that rewritten domain, while the explicit domain the method passed to `getSolidityVerifierParameters` reached the config unchanged. `enrollInput` and `signingInput` now throw a `TypeError` with the new `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_DOMAIN_MESSAGE` for a non-empty domain the rule changes (`https://recover.example`, `Recover.example`, `recover.example/`), after the missing-domain-or-scope check; nothing is rewritten, so the domain given, the stack's, the verifier parameters' and the committed config's are one string. `configFrom` answers `material-rejected` for an input record whose domain the rule changes, for a proof holding fewer than `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SCOPED_MIN_PUBLIC_INPUTS` (8) public inputs, and for one whose inputs `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SERVICE_SCOPE_INPUT` (3) and `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SERVICE_SUBSCOPE_INPUT` (4) are not the scope hashes of the session's domain and scope (the first `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SCOPE_HASH_BYTES`, 31, bytes of the text's UTF-8 `sha256` as an integer, zero for the empty text); it commits the session's domain and scope instead of the stack's echo. `replyFrom` answers the new frozen record `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_REQUEST_UNSUPPORTED` (`request-unsupported`), before loading the stack and after its existing checks (an input that is not a session, material without an outer proof, or a digest or config that does not decode still answers `material-rejected` first), for a config whose domain the rule changes or whose domain or scope is empty, and `material-rejected` for a proof whose two scope words are not the config's hashes; `verify` answers `rejected` for such a proof, still without calling the stack. New constants: `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SERVICE_SCOPE_INPUT`, `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SERVICE_SUBSCOPE_INPUT`, `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SCOPED_MIN_PUBLIC_INPUTS`, `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_SCOPE_HASH_BYTES`, `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_REQUEST_UNSUPPORTED`, `METHOD_ZKPASSPORT_DOMAIN_MESSAGE`. No type, closed set or record shape changes.

Breaking: the zkPassport `enrollInput` and `signingInput` throw on domains they accepted, and its `configFrom`, `replyFrom` and `verify` refuse proofs they passed: one with fewer than eight public inputs, or whose service scope or subscope word is not the hash of the config's domain or scope.

A credential already committed under a domain the stack rewrites can never approve: the committed domain is part of the credential, while every proof carries the hash of the rewritten domain. No migration exists, and `replyFrom` answers `request-unsupported` for it. Re-enroll the credential under the domain the stack keeps (`recover.example` for `https://recover.example/`) and commit the new setup while the account still holds a key; an account that holds none cannot replace it.
