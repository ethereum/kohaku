An Aadhaar proving call now forwards no progress after its signal aborts or the call settles, and the method documents what an abort stops. The Aadhaar method hands the proving stack the page's `onProgress` gated on the material's `signal` and on the call, so a state reaches the page only until the signal aborts or the call settles with a proof or a failure, and never while the signal throws; an injected stack receives that gated callback as the second argument of `AnonAadhaarStack.prove`, beside the signal as before, and a state it reports through the callback after the call settled reaches nothing. The JSDoc of `AadhaarMaterial.signal`, `AnonAadhaarStack.prove`, `configFrom`, `replyFrom` and `anonAadhaarMethod` states the contract: an abort before or during any step settles that call `device-refused` at once, that call returns no proof or config, and no progress is forwarded after the signal aborts or the call settles. The SDK starts no worker, so the installed `@anon-aadhaar/core` stack's download and proving are not stopped and hold their memory and CPU until they end. A call started meanwhile over the same files proves alongside them; one over other files waits for them to end. An injected stack receives the signal and may stop its own work. `anonAadhaarMethod` moved unchanged into its own source file and stays exported from the `./recovery-methods` entry as before. No record, closed set, constant, device fact or signature changes.

Compatibility: a page's `onProgress` is no longer called after its signal aborts or the call settles, so a later step's status is no longer overwritten by an aborted proof or by an injected stack that keeps the callback. An injected stack whose `prove` compared the callback it receives with the page's own by identity now receives a wrapper. A page with no `onProgress` sees no change.
